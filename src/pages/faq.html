FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about commissioning. The full legal detail lives in the terms of service.

Tap a question to open it. Something not covered here? Ask directly on Instagram or use the commission form.

1How do I request a quote?

Fill in the commission form on the Contact page, or send a direct message on Instagram. Every quote stays valid for 60 days.

2How do I secure a commission slot?

A non-refundable deposit secures your slot. Payment plans are available if you need one — ask when you request your quote.

3When do I pay the rest?

Full payment is due before creation starts. The exact schedule is in the terms of service.

4Will I see my piece while it is being made?

Yes — you receive work-in-progress updates while your piece is created, so you can follow along from first sketch to finished suit.

5Who pays shipping?

Shipping is covered by you, the client. Details are in the terms of service.

6What do you make?

Custom fursuits as a head only, mini partial, partial or full suit, plus fursona design and VRChat and VTuber avatar creation. Premade parts — like paws — are announced on Instagram first.

7How long does a commission take?

Turnaround depends on the queue and the complexity of your design. Ask about the current estimated timeframe when you request your quote.
